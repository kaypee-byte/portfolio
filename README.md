# Kwanele P. Ntshalintshali — Personal Portfolio

Welcome to my portfolio. This website brings together my projects, experiences, and learning in one place. It gives visitors a chance to explore the work I’m doing across cybersecurity, software engineering, and project management, and to follow how that work develops over time.

The portfolio is designed to grow with me. I can add new projects, photos, videos, and reflections as I learn and take on new challenges. Visitors can browse the published content and get a clearer picture of the ideas I’m exploring and the problems I’m working to solve.

## Explore the portfolio

### Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking
This section follows my developing interest in cybersecurity and ethical hacking. It can include learning notes, practice labs, capture-the-flag challenges, and reflections on what I discover along the way. The focus is on learning and using security knowledge responsibly.

### Software Engineering
Here I share websites and software projects, including what each project is for, what I contributed, and what I learned while building it. Where available, projects include screenshots, technologies used, and links to the code.

### Project Management
This section highlights the planning and teamwork behind projects: coordinating tasks, tracking progress, communicating with others, and working towards shared goals. It may also include milestones and lessons from working with Beacon Innovation Hub.

## A portfolio that keeps moving forward

This site is more than a collection of finished projects. It is also a record of learning in progress. Some work may be complete, while other ideas are still being explored. New entries will be added as projects develop and I gain experience.

The design uses interactive features and animation to make browsing more engaging, while keeping the content easy to read on phones, tablets, and computers.
